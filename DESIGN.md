## Brand & Style

The design system powers the mission-critical legal operations of Pakistan's premier national energy corporation. It serves corporate legal counsel, executive board members, external litigators, and compliance officers handling high-stakes corporate arbitration, concession disputes, labor matters, and regulatory tribunals. 

The aesthetic is **Corporate / Modern** precision engineered for the institutional energy sector. It combines structural authority, absolute data clarity, and ergonomic operational rhythm. The interface instills calm confidence under legal pressure through crisp structural boundaries, unambiguous informational density, deep oceanic slate anchors, and vitalizing mineral teals that signify institutional stewardship.

Visual posture:
- **Authoritative & Institutional:** Dignified dark navy shells framing crisp porcelain workspaces, avoiding frivolous consumer-grade trends in favor of disciplined operational focus.
- **Vigilant & Decisive:** Visual priority is engineered around statutory deadlines, bench appearances, jurisdictional hierarchies, and fiscal liability metrics.
- **Tactile Enterprise Craft:** Subtle hairline structural dividers (`#E2E8F0`), measured inner radii, and balanced micro-surfaces that prevent visual fatigue over 10-hour analytical shifts.

## Typography

The typographical pairing strikes an intentional dialectic between statutory governance and tabular efficiency:

- **Executive & Display Tier (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Applied exclusively to view headers, litigation track titles, high-level aggregated liability figures, and modal banners. Its contemporary geometric curves relieve the tension of complex legal copy without degrading authority.
- **Operational & Tabular Tier (Inter):** Deployed across case dossiers, court order citations, multi-column matter indices, filter bars, and statutory timelines. Inter’s open counters, tall x-height, and extensive OpenType feature support (`tnum`, `cv05`, `calt`) guarantee legible docket numbers, case years (`W.P. No. 4921/2023`), and monetary values under dense presentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a persistent high-productivity workspace model:

### Structural Framework
- **Master Navigation Rail:** Fixed left navigation dock (`#0D1F2D`) with default width of `260px`, collapsing into an icon-rail (`72px`) for data-heavy split-pane review.
- **Main Canvas Grid:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid configured with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `1.75rem` (`28px`) canvas margins on high-resolution displays (`≥1280px`).
- **Data Workspaces:** Fixed max-width of `1920px` to prevent multi-column distortion on ultra-wide legal terminal setups.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Logic
- **Desktop (≥1280px):** Full dual-pane litigation layout: dynamic master list / timeline preview alongside active docket dossiers. Multi-column metric ribbons span all 12 columns.
- **Tablet / Laptop (1024px – 1279px):** Sidebar converts to iconography rail; 12-column grid scales to `1rem` gutters; secondary analytical charts stack below hearing dockets.
- **Field / Handheld (<1024px):** Navigation tucks into a secure dark canvas sheet; grid collapses to single-column stacking with `1rem` safe-edge padding; dense data tables switch to contextual card lists featuring priority metadata (Cause Title, Forum, Next Date).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layering anchored by hairline boundaries** and softened with low-luminance ambient shadows. Surfaces do not float arbitrarily; they sit directly in distinct functional strata:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base - `#F5F7FA`):** Inactive system ground upon which components rest.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Tier - `#FFFFFF`):** High-density modules bounded by a single pixel border in `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(13, 31, 45, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(13, 31, 45, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Analytical & Interactive Focus Cards):** KPI metric counters, active hearing cards, and filter panels. Shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 31, 45, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(13, 31, 45, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Command Palettes & Pinned Drawers):** Lateral case-detail sheets and global search indices. Shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(13, 31, 45, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -4px rgba(13, 31, 45, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Statutory Modals & Critical Overlays):** Interlocutory injunction warnings and case disposal prompts. Shadow: `0 20px 36px -8px rgba(13, 31, 45, 0.18)`. Backdrop: `rgba(13, 31, 45, 0.65)` with a `4px` blur filter.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, controlled radii (`roundedness: 2`) to balance structural corporate authority with modern operational ease. 

- **Cards, Main Dashboards & Modals:** Standardized corner radius of `10px` to `12px` (`rounded-lg`), delivering crisp containment without harsh industrial corners.
- **Controls & Form Fields:** Standardized corner radius of `8px` (`rounded-md`), pairing cleanly with adjacent table cells.
- **Status Badges, Legal Stage Indicators & Pills:** Fully rounded capsule format (`9999px`), distinguishing informational classification tags from actionable rectangular buttons.

## Components

### 1. Primary, Secondary & Utility Buttons
- **Primary Action (File Pleadings / Add Case):** Solid `#36B388` fill, white typography (`label-md`), 8px border radius, 36px height for tables, 42px height for main action bars. Hover: `#23946F`. Focus ring: 3px `#36B38833`.
- **Corporate Blue Action (Appellate / Transfer Actions):** Solid `#1F6096` fill, white text, 8px radius. Hover: `#13436B`.
- **Secondary / Outline (Export Cause List / Filter):** 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, white background, `#1E293B` text. Hover: `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Critical / Destructive (Flag Contempt / Strike Out):** Translucent crimson background (`#FEF2F2`), `#EF4444` solid border, `#EF4444` label.

### 2. Legal Data Tables
- **Structural Integrity:** Pinned zero-elevation white header with uppercase micro-labels (`label-sm`, color: `#64748B`), bottom hairline divider `#CBD5E1`.
- **Row Architecture:** Alternating subtle row zebra striping (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`), row height at 48px standard (38px high-density switch). Cell typography: `body-sm` with tabular numerals enabled.
- **Hover & Selection:** Row hover shifts background to `#EBF3FA` (Petroleum Blue tint) with a 3px active primary accent bar sliding along the leftmost boundary.

### 3. KPI Metric Badges & Analytical Stat Tiles
- **Structure:** Level 1 elevation container with an integrated trend indicator. Upper row: metric category label (`label-sm`, `#64748B`). Middle: primary value formatted in `headline-xl` (e.g., "PKR 4.2B" or "184 Active Cases").
- **Stat Accents:** Trailing sub-badge indicating urgency: e.g., "12 Listed Today" with a `#FFFBEB` background and `#F59E0B` text.

### 4. Legal Hearing Timeline Cards
- **Visual Path:** Continuous vertical connective spine (`2px` solid `#E2E8F0`) threaded through chronological hearing nodes.
- **Nodes:** Active upcoming dates feature an illuminated `#36B388` ring with an internal `#FFFFFF` core; historical hearings use a solid `#1F6096` or `#CBD5E1` dot.
- **Card Content:** Compact Level 1 surface containing Court Forum (e.g., "High Court of Sindh"), Honorable Bench identification, Hearing Intent, and pinned Counselor initials.

### 5. Status Pills & Jurisdictional Chips
- **Status Tokens:** 24px height, horizontal padding 10px, capsule radius (`rounded-full`), `label-sm`.
  - *Stay Granted / Ad Interim:* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
  - *Decreed in Favor:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
  - *Adverse Judgment:* Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#B91C1C`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.
  - *In Progress / Arguments:* Background `#EBF3FA`, text `#1F6096`, border `1px solid #BAE6FD`.

### 6. Search & Form Inputs
- **Inputs:** 40px default height, surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `0 12px`. Subtle placeholder in `#94A3B8`. Active focus shifts border to `#1F6096` with an omnidirectional focus shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(31, 96, 150, 0.15)`.
- **Global Docket Search:** Integrated within the dark executive top banner (`#132A3E`), with a semi-opaque background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), white text, and `#4ECDA0` icon highlights.
